Frequently Asked Questions

List of frequently asked questions and how to get additional help.

I am running Camunda Local for some time now and want to do a “reset” (i.e., remove all of the deployed assets bpmn, dmn, instances etc). Is it possible?

During the learning or development phase, it is quite normal for us to clear the “slate” and start afresh. Below are the steps to do exactly this.

Please make sure you follow the steps as outlined below. Leaving any steps in between may lead to unexpected errors.

  • Make sure to stop the Camunda Local services, before proceeding with next steps.
  • Zeebe component
    • Delete two folders “data” and “logs”: $home/c8run/camunda-zeebe-8.6.2/data & $home/c8run/camunda-zeebe-8.6.2/logs
  • Elastic component
    • Delete two folders “data” and “logs”: $home/c8run/elasticsearch-8.13.4/data & $home/c8run/elasticsearch-8.13.4/logs
  • Start the Camunda Local script to bring the services up.
    • Verify that all components are up and running.
Can this program be customised and delivered for a private group or organisation?

Yes, this program can be customised to suite specific objectives and needs of your organisational initiatives. Please reach out to us at contact@makelabs.in to discuss further details.

What is the schedule and duration of the program?

The program typically spans 4 days, with about 6 to 7 hours of time spent each day.

Is the content on the portal the same as what is covered in the course?

Each day begins with a set of questions reviewing topics from previous sessions to reinforce learning.

We make the course interactive by using tools like Slido and include take-home assignments to sharpen learnings for all participants.

The material on the portal will be revised on a continuous basis, but the general coverage remains intact.

Who delivers the course and can get to know the details?

The course content is designed by a team of experts with deep industry expertise in working with global organisations and delivering solutions on the latest tech stack.

The coordinator will share the specific details with you upon receiving your interest. Our goal is to partner with teams and organisations to delivery continuous learning.

How long will the participants gain access to the portal and online material?

The portal access will be valid for a duration of one month, starting from the day of commencement of the planned sessions. Participants typically use their official email id to gain access to the portal.

We would like to train our team to build the capability but subsequently use internal methods to train others. Do you support that model?

Yes. We can deliver the initial train the trainer with your select team members. After that, you can get a subscription to gain access to the portal for a period of 1 year. During this time, your teams continue to gain access to all of the online material. The subscription needs to be renewed on yearly basis.

Yes. We offer subscription model to grant your organisation (i.e., all team members from your organisation) access to the portal for a period of 1 year.
